## 💫 About me

I'm not just enthusiastic about AI. I'm obsessed with it, and I ship one thing at a time, in public.

- 🧠 **Machine learning research** at UNC PRIMES: deep learning for brain imaging.
- 📈 **Founder of [Al9ha (Nexus Tensor Alpha)](https://al9ha.vercel.app/)**: AI-native quant research with a Mixture-of-Experts forecasting stack and a multi-LLM agent layer.
- 🛠️ **Quant developer at MeridianAlgo**: building the open-source [Apex Analysis](https://apexanalysis.streamlit.app/) platform.
- 🏆 Captained the only all-high-school team to the finals at the **Duke SCAI Hackathon**.
- 🎯 Where I'm going: algorithm developer, trader, hedge fund manager.
- ⛷️ Off the keyboard: skiing, or standing in the snow at 3am watching the northern lights.
